Welcome to Melodica Trainer

This is your Suzuki M-37C Plus on screen: 37 keys, F3 to F6, low end at the mouthpiece. Type a chord progression, pick a scale, or run a drill, and the keys to press light up. Tap any key to hear the real instrument.

Explore
Type a progression (Am F C G, or Roman numerals like I V vi IV in the current key) or a melody (C D E C) and press Set, or tap the chord chips, or hit Random. Then Play steps through it in time: each chord's keys light with fingering numbers, and the next chord ghosts in one beat early so your hand can move.
Chords
A chord finder: any chord symbol, with inversions and octave placement, and every place it fits on the keyboard.
Drill
Short practice rounds toward the goals below: find notes, play chords, play a progression in time, play back a melody. Answer on screen, on the computer keyboard, or by playing the melodica into the microphone.
Options
Labels fade from every key, to C and F landmarks, to none. Names switches note names to scale degrees. View rotates the keyboard the way it looks in your hands. Air is breath: it darkens and softens the sound like blowing less.
Goals
  1. Given a note name, press every key with that name in under 3 seconds.
  2. Given a triad, play a root-position voicing in the middle register in under 5 seconds.
  3. Play a progression in time at 60 BPM with smooth voicings and at most one miss per cycle.
  4. Play back a short melody at 60 BPM with at most one wrong note.
  5. Play a major scale one octave up and down with standard fingering.
  6. Given a 7th chord, play a voicing in under 5 seconds.
